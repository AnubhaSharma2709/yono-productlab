# YONO ProductLab — Product Analytics Case Study

## 1. Project Overview

YONO ProductLab is a simulated digital banking product analytics case study. It examines how customers interact with a mobile banking application, from onboarding and login to feature discovery and transaction completion.

All users, events, transactions and findings will be synthetic. This project is not based on confidential SBI YONO customer data or internal production metrics.

## 2. Business Context

A digital banking product team needs to understand whether customers can successfully discover and use important features of its mobile application.

High app usage alone does not guarantee a good customer experience. Customers may sign up but fail to activate their accounts, repeatedly encounter errors, abandon payment journeys or stop using the application after their first session.

Product analytics can help identify these patterns and inform improvements.

## 3. Business Problem

The product team lacks a consolidated view of the simulated customer journey across onboarding, authentication, feature engagement and transaction completion.

The analysis will investigate where users drop out of key journeys, which features they adopt, how engagement changes over time and where potential usability or reliability problems may exist.

## 4. Project Objectives

- Measure onboarding and activation funnel conversion.
- Analyse engagement with important banking features.
- Calculate user retention using clearly defined cohort rules.
- Examine transaction initiation, completion and failure patterns.
- Identify differences across acquisition channels, devices and customer segments.
- Recommend product improvements supported by analytical evidence.

## 5. Intended Stakeholders

- Product Manager
- Product Analyst
- Digital Banking Operations Team
- Customer Experience Team

## 6. Expected Deliverables

- Documented synthetic dataset and data dictionary.
- SQL scripts for data validation and product analysis.
- Python notebook for cleaning and exploratory data analysis.
- Defined product KPIs and calculation rules.
- Power BI dashboard.
- Final analytical report with findings, limitations and recommendations.

## 7. Scope and Limitations

This is an educational portfolio project using simulated data. Results will demonstrate analytical methods and will not represent actual SBI YONO user behaviour, business performance or customer outcomes.

Any proposed product changes will be recommendations for further investigation, not claims of proven business impact.
